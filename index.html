FAQ

How do I use charge shots, and what are the different tiers?
Hold down the [J] or [C] key to charge your weapon. Letting go early fires tiered charged shots (Damage 2 or 4), while holding it until the charge spark and audio cue trigger gives you a powerful 8-damage max charge shot.
Why isn't my dash working immediately when I press Shift?
Holding [SHIFT] + [A/D] charges up a high-speed dash for about 1.2 seconds. Changing directions while holding Shift won't cancel your charge, but you must keep holding until the burst triggers automatically.
I messed up my speedrun time. Can I restart quickly?
Yes! If you are playing in Speedrun Mode, you can press [R] at any time to instantly reset your run back to Level 1.
Why do tarantulas have 1 health?
Because they are ugly.
I am stuck on Core Defense. How do I beat it?
Core Defense gets intense fast! I recommend saving up your scrap rather than spending it right away, so you can afford the powerful Overcharge upgrade (25 Scrap) during the first upgrade window.
